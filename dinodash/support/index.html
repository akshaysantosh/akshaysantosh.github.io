DinoDash — Support

Tap anywhere on the start screen to begin, and tap to jump. Use the arrows to choose your dino.

How do I play?

Your dino runs on its own. Tap anywhere (or the arrow button) to jump over asteroids on the ground — but don’t jump into the ones floating in the air.

What does the roar button do?

Every 100 points it lights up. Tap it to clear every asteroid on screen for a bonus.

How do I turn the music off?

Tap the music-note button on the start screen or at the top of the game. Sound effects stay on.

Is the difficulty different for each dino?

No. Every dino plays exactly the same — only its world and roar look different.
